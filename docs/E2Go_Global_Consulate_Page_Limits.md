### 1.1 What This Table Shows

Every U.S. consulate sets its own page limits, tab structure, submission format, and exempt document rules independently. There is no universal standard across all 82 treaty countries — each post publishes its own requirements, and they change without notice.

The table below is organized by confirmed page limit, from most restrictive to most generous.

**Important disclaimer for E2Go:** Page limits and submission requirements change regularly and without advance notice. This table must be treated as a living reference. The app must always display: *"Verify your consulate's current requirements at [post URL] before submitting your final package."*

## SECTION 2: THE NETHERLANDS / ITALY MODEL — TAB-SPECIFIC LIMITS

### 2.1 Why This Is Different

The Netherlands (Amsterdam) and Italy (Rome) do not set a single overall page limit — they set **individual page limits by tab**. This is a fundamentally different constraint and requires a different allocation strategy.

**Amsterdam and Rome confirmed tab limits:**

| Tab | Section | Max Pages | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tab A | Table of Contents | Not specified | Brief — typically 1 page |
| Tab B | Forms | Not specified | DS-160, DS-156E, G-28 — standard admin docs |
| Tab C | Applicant Information | Not specified | CV, passport, family docs, intent statement |
| Tab D | Ownership | Not specified | Corporate docs, operating agreement, org chart |
| **Tab E** | **Investment** | **40 pages maximum** | Cover letter, investment evidence, source of funds, lease, franchise agreement — THIS is where the cover letter lives at Amsterdam |
| **Tab F** | **Real and Operating** | **20 pages maximum** | Business licenses, bank statements, invoices, photos of premises |
| **Tab G** | **Marginality** | **20 pages maximum** | Business plan, tax returns (if existing), P&L, W-2s, hiring plan |

**Total maximum if all tabs are at their cap:** approximately 80 pages across the entire package (Tabs E + F + G = 80 pages; Tabs A–D are uncapped).

**Key structural difference at Amsterdam:** The cover letter goes in **Tab E**, not Tab A as in most other posts. Tab A at Amsterdam is only the Table of Contents.

### 2.2 Business Plan Allocation at Amsterdam / Rome

Tab G holds all the non-marginality evidence. At 20 pages maximum for the entire tab, the business plan budget is:

| Item in Tab G | Pages |
|---|---|
| Business plan (full or compressed) | 8–12 pages |
| 5-year financial projections | 2–3 pages |
| Job creation / hiring plan | 1 page |
| Non-marginality table | 1 page |
| Tax returns (if existing business — 2 years, pages 1–2) | 4 pages |
| W-2s and 1099s | 2–3 pages |
| **Total** | **~18–24 pages** — must be compressed to fit 20-page cap |

**Strategy at Amsterdam/Rome:** Existing businesses have a harder time fitting Tab G because they must include actual tax returns and W-2s alongside the business plan. New/startup businesses have more room since they only need the forward-looking plan.

## SECTION 3: THE UK MODEL — NO PAGE LIMIT, 20 MB FILE CONSTRAINT

### 3.1 The 20 MB Reality

London has no page limit, but a 20 MB file size cap on the uploaded PDF [page:0]. At standard PDF compression settings:

| Scenario | Approximate File Size |
|---|---|
| 50 pages, text-heavy, no photos | ~3–5 MB |
| 50 pages, mixed text and scanned documents | ~8–12 MB |
| 80 pages, scanned bank statements and leases | ~15–18 MB |
| 100 pages with photos of business premises | ~18–22 MB — approaching or over limit |

**Strategy at London:** The effective constraint is file size, not page count. The business plan can be written at full length (16–25 pages for the full version). However, **scanned documents must be compressed** — bank statements, leases, and invoices scanned at 300 DPI can be large. The E2Go PDF exporter should auto-compress scanned documents to 150 DPI for London submissions to stay within 20 MB.

### 3.2 London Submission Process

Unlike most posts, London does not accept email submissions. After booking the interview, the embassy sends a link for the applicant to **upload the package via an online portal** [page:0]. E2Go should note this in the submission instructions for UK-based applicants.

## SECTION 4: ISRAEL — THE 150-PAGE OUTLIER

Israel's Tel Aviv embassy has the most generous numeric limit at 150 pages, with a notably broad list of exemptions [web:160]:

**Exempt at Israel (not counted toward 150 pages):**
- DS-160, DS-156E, G-28
- CV / resume
- Marriage and birth certificates
- All Hebrew-to-English translations

The practical result is that even a very complex partnership or multi-document case can be filed without compression at Tel Aviv. However, practitioners still recommend keeping the package lean — officers reading 150 pages take longer than officers reading 50 pages, and unnecessary padding does not help the case.

## SECTION 6: THE THIRD-COUNTRY NATIONAL (TCN) FACTOR

### 6.1 What Is a TCN Application?

A Third-Country National applies for an E-2 visa at a consulate NOT in their home country. For example:

- A **Canadian citizen** applying at **Frankfurt** (because they live or are temporarily in Germany)
- A **Dutch citizen** applying at **London**
- A **Turkish citizen** applying at **Toronto**

TCN applications are common in the E-2 world, but not all posts welcome them equally.

### 6.2 TCN Friendliness by Post

| Post | TCN Friendliness | Practical Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Toronto** | ★★★ (Score 3/3) | Very TCN-friendly; actively accepts TCN applications |
| **London** | ★★ (Score 2/3) | Regularly processes TCN applications |
| **Sydney** | ★★ (Score 2/3) | Regularly processes TCN applications |
| **Melbourne** | ★★ (Score 2/3) | Regularly processes TCN applications |
| **Frankfurt** | ★★ (Score 2/3) | Processes TCN applications; 30-page limit applies regardless of nationality |
| **Belfast** | ★ (Score 1/3) | Limited TCN processing; may require justification |
| **Other posts** | Varies | Contact post directly; TCN policy can change without notice |

### 6.3 Why This Matters for E2Go

A Canadian citizen who is abroad at the time of application may want to apply at London or Frankfurt instead of Toronto. The page limit at that post applies regardless of the applicant's nationality. E2Go should present TCN options to Canadian applicants who indicate they are not in Canada, and immediately load the page budget for the selected post.

## SECTION 7: WHAT TO DO WHEN THE POST IS UNKNOWN OR UNVERIFIED

### 7.1 The Default Assumption

If a user has not selected a consulate, or if their consulate's page limit is not confirmed in E2Go's database, the app defaults to the **50-page Toronto model** — the most common limit for high-volume posts — and displays a warning:

> ⚠️ *"Your consulate's specific page limit has not been verified in our database. We are using the standard 50-page model as a default. Before finalizing your application package, confirm your consulate's current requirements at [consulate URL]."*

### 7.2 The Verification Checklist

E2Go provides a pre-submission consulate verification checklist:

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Current page limit | Visit the consulate's E-visa page and look for the E-2 instructions PDF |
| Exempt documents list | Check which documents do not count toward the limit at your post |
| Tab structure | Confirm the tab labels (A–G, A–H, or other) and what goes in each tab |
| Submission method | Email, online portal, or physical mail |
| File size limit | Confirm maximum email attachment or upload size |
| TCN policy | If applying outside home country, confirm the post accepts TCN applications |
| Appointment scheduling | Confirm whether documents are submitted before or after booking the interview |

### 7.3 The Cover Letter Strategy for Unknown Posts

When the post is unverified, the safest strategy for the cover letter is to assume it **does count toward the page limit** (as at Toronto) and write it in a concise 4–5 page format. If it later turns out the cover letter is exempt (as at Frankfurt), the saved pages are a bonus.

## SECTION 8: HOW THE AMSTERDAM / NETHERLANDS TAB STRUCTURE CHANGES EVERYTHING

### 8.1 The Cover Letter Is in Tab E, Not Tab A

This is one of the most confusing post-specific differences in the E-2 world. At the U.S. Consulate General in Amsterdam, the **cover letter — including the full legal argument addressing all 6 E-2 eligibility requirements — is placed inside Tab E: Investment**, not in Tab A [page:0].

Tab A at Amsterdam is only the Table of Contents.

This means:
- Tab E is doing double duty: it holds both the legal argument (cover letter) AND all the investment documentation
- At 40 pages maximum for Tab E, the cover letter (4–5 pages) and investment docs (8–12 pages) must both fit within 40 pages — this is generally manageable
- The investor's CV and qualifications go in **Tab C: Applicant Information** at Amsterdam (not Tab H as at Toronto)

### 8.2 "Think Lean" — The Amsterdam Philosophy

The Amsterdam consulate's official instructions include a remarkable editorial comment at the end of the published PDF:

> *"Do not include brochures or business plans that contribute little or nothing to the value of your case. We evaluate many applications monthly. Please think lean, and demonstrate your business prowess."*

This is an explicit instruction from the consulate to be concise. Any padding, marketing materials, or repetitive documentation is not just wasteful — it is contrary to the post's stated preferences. E2Go should display this guidance to all Amsterdam applicants.

### 9.2 Compression Mode by Post

E2Go uses four compression modes, automatically assigned by consulate:

| Mode | Assigned To | Business Plan | Other Documents |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NONE** | London, Sydney, Melbourne, Israel | Full version (16–22 pages) | Full versions of all docs |
| **STANDARD** | Toronto, Paris, Madrid, Lisbon, Amsterdam, Rome | 10–13 pages | Key pages of lease, franchise agreement |
| **COMPRESSED** | Paraguay | 14–18 pages | Minor compression of narrative |
| **MAXIMUM** | Frankfurt | 6–8 pages | Maximum compression; cover letter and CV in exempt zone |

### 9.3 PDF File Size Management (London and Portal-Submission Posts)

For posts with file size limits instead of page limits, E2Go applies automatic PDF optimization:

- Scanned documents: down-sample to 150 DPI (from typical 300 DPI scan)
- Business plan (text-generated): compress fonts and remove embedded metadata
- Color photos of premises: compress to 72 DPI / JPEG quality 60%
- After optimization: display estimated file size and alert if still > 18 MB (2 MB safety margin)

## SECTION 10: THE CRITICAL WARNING — LIMITS CHANGE WITHOUT NOTICE

The Toronto limit dropped from 70 to 50 pages in mid-2024 with very little advance notice, and Frankfurt dropped from 90 pages to 30 pages in a similarly abrupt manner [web:135][web:133]. The LinkedIn post from immigration attorney James Hollis noted at the time of the Toronto reduction:

> *"Toronto is one of the highest volume E visa units in the world and this change should allow them to continue to process efficiently. However, documentation limits like this can be a double-edged sword: fewer documents initially may require additional requests for evidence if the source of funds or commitment of the investment can't be done in that limited page count."*

This is the single most important operational risk for E2Go: a client builds their package to 50 pages for Toronto, and the limit silently drops to 40 before submission.

**E2Go mitigation:**
1. Display the date each post's limit was last verified
2. Include a pre-export checklist item: "Confirm current page limit at [consulate URL] before submitting"
3. Build the package with a 10% buffer below the stated limit wherever possible
4. Maintain a "consulate alerts" log — when a practitioner or official source reports a change, update the database and notify all users with packages in progress at that post
