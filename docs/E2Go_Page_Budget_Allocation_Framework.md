# E2GO — PAGE BUDGET ALLOCATION FRAMEWORK
## Consulate-Specific Page Limits and Document Compression Strategy
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** June 22, 2026
**Status:** Active — Knowledge Base
**Authority:** U.S. Consulate Toronto (ca.usembassy.gov), U.S. Consulate Frankfurt (fr.usembassy.gov), U.S. Embassy Spain (es.usembassy.gov), practitioner-confirmed limits
**Module:** Document Builder / PDF Package Generator / Application Confidence Engine
**Purpose:** Defines the page budget for each consulate post, what counts toward the limit, what is exempt, and exactly how to allocate the available pages across the tabbed binder. Provides two full allocation models — one for Toronto (50 pages) and one for Frankfurt (30 pages) — plus a compression decision framework for what to cut when space is tight.

---

## SECTION 1: CONSULATE PAGE LIMITS BY POST

### 1.1 Key Post Reference Table

| Consulate | Page Limit | Exempt from Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Toronto, Canada** | **50 pages** (new cases only) | DS-160 confirmation, DS-156E, G-28, civil documents (birth/marriage certificates), passport bio-pages, tab dividers | Reduced from 70 in mid-2024; renewals are EXEMPT from the page limit entirely |
| **Frankfurt, Germany** | **30 pages** | Cover letter, DS-156E, DS-160, G-28, applicant qualifications (CV, skills letters) | Reduced from 90 pages; some of the most critical narrative docs are now EXCLUDED from the count |
| **Paris, France** | **50 pages** | Tabs A–C and G-28 excluded from count | Confirmed at 50 pages with Tabs A–C exempt |
| **Madrid, Spain** | **50 pages** | Standard exempt categories | E-visa unit confirmed via PDF guidelines |
| **Mexico City, Mexico** | **100 pages** | Standard exempt categories | Highest limit among major E-2 posts |
| **Other posts** | **30–100 pages** | Varies by post | Always verify directly with the specific consulate before submitting |

**Critical rule:** Page limits and exempt categories change without notice. E2Go should always direct users to verify current limits at their specific consulate BEFORE generating the final PDF package.

---

### 1.2 What "Exempt" Means in Practice

Documents that are EXEMPT from the page count do not consume your budget — but they still must be included in the binder. The exemption is purely about arithmetic. This distinction is important because it changes the strategy:

**At Toronto**, the most space-consuming administrative documents (DS-160, DS-156E, civil documents, passport copies, tab dividers) are exempt. This means the 50 pages are reserved entirely for the substantive case documents — investment evidence, business plan, financial projections, and company structure.

**At Frankfurt**, the most space-consuming NARRATIVE documents (cover letter, applicant CV, skills letters) are exempt. This means the 30 pages are reserved for the hard documentary evidence — financial records, investment proof, corporate documents, and business plan financials.

This is a fundamental difference in philosophy between the two posts. Toronto wants the narrative squeezed; Frankfurt wants the evidence squeezed.

## SECTION 2: TORONTO — 50-PAGE ALLOCATION MODEL

### 2.1 Exempt Documents (DO NOT count against the 50 pages)

The following are confirmed exempt at Toronto:

| Document | Typical Pages |
|---|---|
| DS-160 confirmation page (each applicant) | 1 per applicant |
| DS-156E form | 2–3 |
| G-28 (Notice of Entry of Appearance, if attorney used) | 1 |
| Civil documents: marriage certificate | 1–2 |
| Civil documents: birth certificates (per child) | 1 each |
| Passport bio-page copies (investor + co-investors) | 1 per person |
| Tab dividers / section labels | Not counted |

These documents are OUTSIDE the 50-page envelope. Do not include them in your page budget calculations.

---

### 2.2 The 50-Page Budget — Toronto Standard Case

This is the allocation for a single Canadian investor, new franchise, no dependents yet, new case (not renewal).

| Tab | Section | Pages Allocated | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Cover Letter | **4–5 pages** | Addresses all 6 E-2 elements; references other tabs by name; dense but critical |
| **C** | Company Structure & Ownership | **3–4 pages** | Articles of organization/formation (2p), operating agreement key pages (1–2p) |
| **D** | Proof of Investment | **8–10 pages** | Wire transfer(s) (1–2p), signed lease (2–3p), franchise agreement key pages (2–3p), equipment invoices (1–2p), use-of-funds table (1p) |
| **E** | Proof the Enterprise Operates | **4–5 pages** | Business license (1p), bank account statements (2p), any operational evidence (1–2p) |
| **F** | Proof of Substantiality | **2–3 pages** | Investment proportionality table (1p), capital commitment letter if needed (1p), total cost breakdown (1p) |
| **G** | Proof Not Marginal (incl. Business Plan) | **12–15 pages** | Business plan (8–10p), 5-year P&L projections (2–3p), hiring schedule (1p), non-marginality table (1p) |
| **H** | Applicant Qualifications | **2–3 pages** | Investor resume (2p), org chart (1p) |
| | **TOTAL** | **35–45 pages** | Leaves 5–15 pages as buffer for complexity |

**The business plan gets roughly 20–30% of the total budget — 10–15 pages maximum at Toronto.** This is consistent with practitioner guidance that the business plan should take approximately 15–20% of the application when a limit is set.

---

### 2.3 Toronto Page Budget — Partnership (Two Co-Investors)

Adding a co-investor adds pages primarily in Tabs C, D, and H.

| Tab | Section | Pages Allocated | Delta vs. Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Cover Letter | **5–6 pages** | +1 page for role differentiation section |
| **C** | Company Structure & Ownership | **4–5 pages** | +1–2 pages for second investor's ownership docs and shareholder agreement |
| **D** | Proof of Investment | **10–12 pages** | +2 pages for second investor's separate investment documentation |
| **E** | Proof the Enterprise Operates | **4–5 pages** | Same as standard |
| **F** | Proof of Substantiality | **2–3 pages** | Same as standard |
| **G** | Proof Not Marginal (Business Plan) | **12–15 pages** | Same as standard — one shared business plan |
| **H** | Applicant Qualifications | **4–5 pages** | +2 pages for second investor's resume and org chart |
| | **TOTAL** | **41–51 pages** | AT OR OVER the 50-page limit — compression required |

**Important:** A partnership case at Toronto almost always hits the 50-page ceiling. This means making hard editorial choices — see Section 4 for the compression decision framework.

---

### 2.4 Toronto Page Budget — Adult Child as Co-Investor (Separate Binder)

Each investor files a SEPARATE binder. Each binder has its own 50-page limit.

**Parent's binder:** Standard 35–45 page model above.
**Adult child's binder:** Same structure, but:
- Tab D must include the promissory note (if parent loaned funds) — adds 1–2 pages
- Tab H must include the adult child's resume and their distinct role description
- The business plan in Tab G is the SAME document as the parent's — but each binder carries its own copy, which counts toward each 50-page limit

The adult child's binder can be slightly leaner because their investment evidence is simpler (fewer purchased assets), but the business plan alone will consume 10–15 pages from each binder's budget.

---

### 2.5 Toronto Page Budget — Renewal

**Renewals are EXEMPT from the 50-page limit at Toronto.** There is no page cap for renewal applications. However, practitioners recommend keeping renewal packages concise — typically 30–50 pages — because officer time is limited and excess pages may delay review rather than help.

Typical renewal package at Toronto:

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| Cover letter (renewal version) | 3–4 |
| DS-156E and administrative | Exempt |
| Updated corporate documents (if changed) | 2–3 |
| Federal tax returns (2 years, pages 1–2 each) | 4 |
| Schedule K (ownership) | 2 |
| IRS Form W-2 (all employees, most recent year) | 2–4 |
| IRS Form 941 (quarterly payroll filing, most recent year) | 4 |
| Business bank statements (current year) | 6–10 |
| Updated financials / P&L | 2–3 |
| Recent invoices or client contracts | 3–5 |
| Updated org chart (if changed) | 1 |
| **Total** | **~30–40 pages** |

## SECTION 3: FRANKFURT — 30-PAGE ALLOCATION MODEL

### 3.1 Exempt Documents at Frankfurt (DO NOT count against the 30 pages)

Frankfurt's exemptions are MORE generous on the narrative side and STRICTER on the documentary side compared to Toronto:

| Document | Confirmed Exempt |
|---|---|
| DS-160 confirmation page | ✅ Exempt |
| DS-156E form | ✅ Exempt |
| G-28 | ✅ Exempt |
| **Cover letter** | ✅ **Exempt** — this is different from Toronto |
| **Applicant qualifications (CV, skills/specialization letters)** | ✅ **Exempt** — this is different from Toronto |
| Company/cover letter | ✅ Exempt |

**Critical difference from Toronto:** The cover letter and the investor's CV/resume are EXEMPT from Frankfurt's 30-page count. This means the 30 pages are almost entirely consumed by hard documentary evidence and the business plan financials.

---

### 3.2 The 30-Page Budget — Frankfurt Standard Case

| Tab | Section | Pages Allocated | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Cover Letter | **EXEMPT** | Does not count; can be 4–6 pages |
| **C** | Company Structure & Ownership | **4–5 pages** | Articles (2p), operating agreement key pages (2p), org chart (1p) |
| **D** | Proof of Investment | **8–10 pages** | Wire transfers (1–2p), signed lease key pages (2p), franchise agreement key pages (2p), equipment invoices (2p), use-of-funds table (1p) |
| **E** | Proof the Enterprise Operates | **3–4 pages** | Business license (1p), bank statements (2p) |
| **F** | Proof of Substantiality | **1–2 pages** | Proportionality table (1p) |
| **G** | Business Plan / Non-Marginality | **7–9 pages** | Compressed business plan — summary sections only; full 5-year P&L (2–3p); hiring table (1p); non-marginality table (1p) |
| **H** | Applicant Qualifications | **EXEMPT** | CV, org chart, and skills letters do not count |
| | **TOTAL** | **23–30 pages** | Very tight — no buffer for complex cases |

**At Frankfurt, the business plan gets roughly 7–9 pages out of the 30 available.** This is dramatically less than Toronto. The business plan must be written in a compressed format — summary paragraphs, tables, and financial schedules only. No lengthy narrative sections.

---

### 3.3 What Gets Cut First at Frankfurt

Because 30 pages is severe, the following documents must be ruthlessly compressed or eliminated:

| Document | Toronto (50p) | Frankfurt (30p) | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business plan narrative | 8–10 pages | 3–4 pages | Cut executive summary; cut market analysis narrative; keep only financial tables + key bullets |
| Lease agreement | 2–3 pages (full) | 1 page (key terms only) | Extract and include only: parties, address, term, rent, signatures |
| Franchise agreement | 2–3 pages (key pages) | 1 page (signature page + fee table only) | Include ONLY signature page and key fee/royalty schedule |
| Financial projections | 2–3 pages | 1–2 pages | Combine 5-year P&L and non-marginality table onto a single page |
| Equipment invoices | 2–3 pages | 1 page (itemized summary table) | Replace individual invoices with a single summary table |
| Bank statements | 2–3 pages | 1–2 pages | Most recent month only; or key pages showing balance and transfers |
| Cover letter | 4–5 pages (counts at Toronto) | EXEMPT (unlimited) | Write a more thorough cover letter at Frankfurt — it's free pages |
| CV / resume | 2 pages (counts at Toronto) | EXEMPT (unlimited) | Full CV at Frankfurt — it's free pages |

## SECTION 4: COMPRESSION DECISION FRAMEWORK

### 4.1 The Priority Hierarchy — What to Protect When Cutting

When pages must be cut, apply this hierarchy: never cut from the top; always cut from the bottom.

**TIER 1 — NEVER CUT (protect at all costs):**
- Wire transfer confirmation(s) — investment proof must be traceable
- Signed lease (at least the key pages: parties, address, term, rent, signatures)
- Non-marginality proof table with projected ratios
- 5-year income statement (at minimum: annual revenue and net income)
- Job creation / hiring schedule
- Corporate ownership structure (operating agreement ownership page)
- All 6 eligibility elements in the cover letter

**TIER 2 — COMPRESS (shorten but do not eliminate):**
- Business plan narrative sections — cut to executive bullets; keep financial tables intact
- Franchise agreement — signature page + key fee schedule only
- Market analysis — replace narrative with 1-page table of local market data
- Investment deployment table — compress to single-page summary
- Equipment invoices — replace individual receipts with single summary table

**TIER 3 — INCLUDE ONLY IF SPACE ALLOWS:**
- Full lease agreement (beyond key pages)
- Full franchise agreement (beyond signature and fee pages)
- Marketing materials (website printout, brochures)
- Supplier contracts
- Advertising evidence
- Additional bank statement months

**TIER 4 — BRING TO THE INTERVIEW (not in the binder):**
- At Toronto: "If you believe additional documents are required to support your application, you can bring them to the interview." This is explicitly permitted by the U.S. Embassy France guidance and widely accepted practice.
- Strategy: anything that falls off the binder due to page limits can be organized into a supplementary folder brought to the interview. Tell the officer in the cover letter: "Additional supporting documentation is available upon request at the interview."

---

### 4.2 Business Plan Compression by Post

The business plan is the largest single document in any binder. Here is how it must be resized by post:

| Section | Full Version (no limit) | Toronto (12–15p budget) | Frankfurt (7–9p budget) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Summary | 1–2 pages | 1 page | ½ page or fold into cover letter |
| Company Description | 1–2 pages | 1 page | ½ page — key facts table only |
| Products and Services | 1–2 pages | 1 page | Fold into company description |
| Market Analysis | 2–4 pages | 1–2 pages | 1 page — table of local data only |
| Marketing Strategy | 1–2 pages | ½–1 page | 3–5 bullets only |
| Operations Plan | 2–3 pages | 1 page | Fold into company description |
| Management Structure | 1–2 pages | ½ page | Exempt at Frankfurt (CV exempt); 3–5 bullets only |
| Financial Plan | 4–6 pages | 3–4 pages | 2 pages — combined P&L + non-marginality table |
| Job Creation Plan | 1 page | 1 page | ½ page — table only, no narrative |
| E-2 Eligibility Summary | 1 page | ½ page | Fold into cover letter (exempt at Frankfurt) |
| **TOTAL** | **~16–25 pages** | **~10–13 pages** | **~6–8 pages** |

---

### 4.3 Page-Count Saving Techniques

These techniques reduce page count without sacrificing substance:

| Technique | Pages Saved | How |
|---|---|---|
| Single-column tables instead of narrative paragraphs | 1–3 pages | Replace prose market analysis with a table of key metrics |
| Reduce font size from 12pt to 11pt | 1–2 pages | Standard for immigration business plans |
| Reduce margins from 1 inch to 0.75 inch | 1 page | Still readable; widely accepted |
| Combine use-of-funds table and substantiality argument onto one page | 1 page | Side-by-side: investment deployed + proportionality ratio |
| Combine non-marginality table and job creation schedule onto one page | 1 page | Two-section single page |
| Extract lease key pages only (parties, term, rent, signatures) | 1–2 pages | Reference full lease as available at interview |
| Extract franchise agreement signature page and fee schedule only | 1–2 pages | Reference full FDD as available at interview |
| Compress 5-year P&L to annual only (no monthly Year 1 in binder) | 1–2 pages | Monthly cash flow can be brought to interview if asked |
| Use bulleted operational plan instead of narrative prose | 1–2 pages | Readable and concise |
| Single-page org chart (auto-generated by E2Go) | — | Already optimized |

When the user selects their consulate post during application setup, E2Go immediately:
1. Loads the confirmed page limit for that post
2. Loads the confirmed exempt document list for that post
3. Sets the document budget mode: STANDARD (≥50 pages), COMPRESSED (30 pages), or EXTENDED (≥100 pages)
4. Adjusts the business plan template to the correct size tier

### 5.3 Budget Alerts

| Alert | Trigger | Message |
|---|---|---|
| 🟡 Approaching limit | > 80% of budget used | "You have used X of your Y available pages. Review Section 4 compression options." |
| 🔴 Over budget | > 100% of budget used | "Your package exceeds the consulate's page limit. The application cannot be submitted. Remove or compress pages before exporting." |
| 🟢 Within budget | ≤ 80% of budget used | "Your package is within the page limit. [X] pages remaining as buffer." |
| ⚡ Business plan too large | Business plan > 30% of total budget | "Your business plan is taking more than 30% of your page budget. Consider compressing the narrative sections." |

### 5.4 One-Click Compression Mode

When the user is over budget, E2Go offers a "Compress for [Post]" mode that:
1. Reduces the business plan to the post-specific compressed template
2. Strips narrative sections from market analysis, operations plan, and services description to bullet-point format
3. Extracts only key pages from uploaded lease and franchise agreement
4. Consolidates financial tables onto fewer pages
5. Re-runs the page count and shows the new total

The user must confirm: "I understand this compression removes detail that may be requested at the interview. I will bring the full versions of these documents to my appointment."
